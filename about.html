Why Pro

Pro is the real-stakes version of the game. You play with Gold, which is backed 1:1 by USDC on Base: deposit USDC, sign and release player shares, earn Tournament Points (TP) and spend them on Player Packs.

  • A curated market of around 140 players from Europe’s five major leagues (the pool can change).
  • Tournaments twice a week, where the top players in each position share the reward pool.
  • Picks: choose a five-player lineup (GK, DEF, MID, FWD, plus a Flex) and try to beat other managers in your division.
