SDF Dashboard – sport.fun player stats

Free player-stats dashboard for sport.fun: points per round, average, form over the last three matches, goals and assists for goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards. Updated after every round. Play the free PitchDuel prediction game or read about the game.

Read-only data. Average = total ÷ number of matches played with points (DNP and empty rounds are excluded). In the “Combined” view the average is calculated across all matches together, not from monthly averages. Dimmed rows have fewer matches played than the minimum. Click a player to see their stats and form.
